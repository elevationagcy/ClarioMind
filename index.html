Frequently Asked Questions

Who is ClarioMind for? expand_more
ClarioMind is designed for high-functioning professionals who want to change their relationship with alcohol. Whether you want to cut back or quit entirely, our tools adapt to your goals.
How is ClarioMind different from other sobriety apps? expand_more
Unlike traditional AA or willpower-based methods, we use neuroscience and cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) techniques. We don't demand labels like "alcoholic" and we focus on the underlying habits.
What if I'm not sure I'm a "high-functioning alcoholic"? expand_more
You don't need a label to benefit. If alcohol is affecting your sleep, focus, or mood, or if you find yourself negotiating rules around drinking, ClarioMind can help you regain clarity.
How is my data protected? expand_more
Your privacy is our top priority. All data is encrypted and anonymized. We never share your personal information with employers or third parties.
